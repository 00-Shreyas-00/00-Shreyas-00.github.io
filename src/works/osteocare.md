## Approach

### Explainable severity grading

OsteoCare analyses knee X-ray images and grades osteoarthritis severity on the Kellgren–Lawrence (KL) scale using an attention-enhanced convolutional neural network, with the aim of supporting early diagnosis. Grad-CAM heatmaps highlight clinically relevant regions such as joint space narrowing and osteophyte formation, so each prediction can be checked visually.

## Platform

### For doctors and patients

The system is deployed as a web-based platform that gives doctors and patients an automated severity prediction along with lifestyle recommendations, including diet and exercise guidance tailored to each grade. Alongside the system, the team wrote a review paper, "A Review Paper on Multimodal AI Solution for Knee Osteoarthritis Severity Grading".
